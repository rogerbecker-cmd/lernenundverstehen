Privacy policy

In short: Lernen & Verstehen does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics or tracking services and no server of our own. Everything the app saves stays on your device.

What is stored on your device

The children’s names (a first name or nickname is enough), picture, school year and world, learning progress, test grades, drawings from the painting studio and settings are stored only in the app on your device. We have no access to them.

If you delete the app, this data is deleted. It may be part of the device backup you have set up yourself with Apple or Google.

Backup code and saved pictures

In the parents’ area you can create a backup code containing the learning progress to transfer it to another device. The code is only displayed; the app does not send it anywhere. The same applies to drawings you save as a picture: they only go where you send them yourself.

Read aloud

To read exercises aloud, the app uses the device’s speech output. The app itself does not transmit any data. The microphone, camera, photos, contacts and location are not used.

Subscription and purchases

The “Lernen & Verstehen Plus” subscription runs exclusively through Apple’s App Store or Google Play. Payment and account data are processed only by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies; we receive no information about you. The app checks the subscription status through the store interface on your device to unlock the content.

The app does not need an internet connection for learning. The internet is only used for the subscription and when you open a link in the parents’ area.

No sharing, no advertising

No data is passed on to third parties. The app contains no advertising, no trackers and no usage analytics libraries. Fonts and images are included in the app and are not loaded from the internet.

Children

The app is made for schoolchildren and their parents. Since it collects no personal data, no data is collected from children either. The parents’ area with the subscription, settings and external links is protected by a maths question.

Contact and your rights

If you have questions about data protection, write to erdwind@live.de. If you send us an email, we use your address and message only to reply and delete them when they are no longer needed. Under the Swiss Data Protection Act and the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification and erasure, and you can lodge a complaint with a data protection authority (in Switzerland, the FDPIC).

Changes

If anything about the app changes that affects data protection, we will update this policy. The version published in the app and on the website applies.
